What counts as an emergency

Call now if you have:

  • Burst or splitting supply line / water that won’t stop
  • Sewer or drain backup into a sink, tub, toilet, or basement
  • Overflowing toilet that won’t stop
  • No water to the house or a major fixture
  • Active leak in a wall, ceiling, or crawlspace
  • No hot water with risk of tank leak or flooding
  • Frozen or iced pipes with risk of burst
  • Gas smell near a water heater or boiler (leave the area and call utility / 911 if needed, then call me for the plumbing follow-up)

If it’s safe, shut the main water valve or the fixture stop. Then call or text (845) 476-9348.

Emergency FAQ — Middletown NY

Do you really run 24/7 emergency in Middletown NY?

Mon–Fri yes — 24/7 emergency. Saturday 9:00 AM–3:00 PM. Sunday closed. Call or text (845) 476-9348.

What should I do before you arrive?

If it’s safe, shut the fixture stop or the main water valve. Keep kids and pets away from standing water. Note where the leak or backup started. Then call or text (845) 476-9348 with what you see.

Can you clear a sewer backup the same visit?

Often yes — snake or clear the line and check whether it’s a fixture clog vs main-line issue. If a camera is needed to confirm damage farther down, I’ll say so before we go further.

Do you handle no hot water and leaking water heaters as emergencies?

Yes. No hot water and actively leaking tanks are emergency calls during the hours above. Repair vs replace is decided on site after diagnosis.

What towns do you cover for emergency plumbing?

Middletown NY first, plus Goshen, Montgomery, Warwick, Monroe, Woodbury, Chester, Circleville, Scotchtown, Mechanicstown, and nearby Orange County townships. Confirm when you call.

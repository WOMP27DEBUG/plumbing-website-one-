Winter drain & freeze tips

  • Know where your main shutoff is before a pipe freezes
  • Let vulnerable lines drip during deep cold snaps
  • Open cabinet doors on exterior walls for sink supply lines
  • Don’t wait on a recurring clog — cold weather makes backups worse overnight
